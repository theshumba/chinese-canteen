食府 · Our Kitchen

A canteen in name.
A kitchen with ambition.

We’re a family kitchen on Bridge Street, a few doors from St John’s and Magdalene. “Canteen” is our promise, not our ceiling — generous, unfussy, made for sharing.

But the cooking reaches for the real thing: the numbing heat of Sichuan, the clean precision of Cantonese, hand-pulled noodles from Henan. Our dumplings are folded by hand each morning, our duck braised and roasted in-house, and we’re one of the only places in Cambridge pulling fresh bubble milk tea.
